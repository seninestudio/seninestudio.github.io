Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Senine Studio LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates applications available on the Apple App Store (the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our Apps, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed to collect as little information as possible. Depending on the specific App, we may collect:

  • Device and usage data — such as device type, operating system version, and general app usage, used only to diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • Information you voluntarily provide — such as content you enter within the App, or details you send us directly via email (e.g., support requests).

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect precise location, contacts, or other sensitive device data unless a specific App explicitly requests it for a core feature, in which case that request is disclosed at the point of use.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Third-Party Services

Our Apps may use limited third-party services (for example, crash reporting or analytics tools) to help us understand app stability and performance. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are contractually restricted from using it for other purposes. We do not control third-party providers' own privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies directly if you have concerns.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information collected. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.
